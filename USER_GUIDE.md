## Glass Product Dashboard – Quick User Guide

This guide explains how to use the dashboard without any technical details.

### Getting Around
- Use the page selector at the top to switch between pages:
  - Homepage
  - Pricing Analysis
  - Assortment Analysis
  - Product Matching
  - Chatbot

### Global Filters (left sidebar)
- Companies: Pick one or more companies to include across the entire dashboard.
- Market Segment: Choose a specific segment or “All Segments”.

These filters affect every page.

---

### Homepage
Purpose: See a quick overview of a single company and compare companies.

What you can do:
- Choose a company (top of page) to view its overview.
- See key metrics: total SKUs, average price, number of categories, and in‑stock items.
- Browse the products table (sortable, filterable columns).
- View Pricing Data: open product expanders to see quantity breaks and per‑item prices.
- Cross‑Company Comparison: compare headline stats across companies.

Local controls:
- Pricing Data filters: capacity bins, price bins, and a search box (SKU/name/quantity). Shows up to 10 matching items—use search to refine.

Tips:
- Use the column headers to sort and filter the table.

---

### Pricing Analysis
Purpose: Understand pricing patterns by size (capacity) and category.

What you can see (open the expanders):
- Average Price by Capacity Range: compare companies by size bands.
- Min–Max Price by Capacity Bin: see price ranges per size band.
- Average Price by Category and Company: compare categories.
- Case Price Tiers (Min vs Max): min/max case prices by size band.

Tips:
- Use the chart toolbar (hover near the top‑right) to zoom or download a PNG image.

---

### Assortment Analysis
Purpose: Explore assortment breadth and coverage.

What you can see (open the expanders):
- SKU Count by Color, Material, Capacity Range.
- Capacity Range Summary table: SKU count, average price, average capacity.
- Market Segment Distribution (pie) and a Company × Segment heatmap.
- Assortment Coverage Metrics: unique colors, materials, shapes, segments, ranges.
- SKU’s by Country of Manufacture (where available).
- Stock Availability Details: counts by stock status.

Tips:
- If something looks empty, adjust the global filters in the sidebar.

---

### Product Matching
Purpose: Find similar products to TricorBraun items, or run a full batch.

Per Item tab:
- Exclude Zero Price Products (checkbox), pick capacity/price bins.
- Choose companies to compare and select a TricorBraun product.
- Click Find Similar Products to see matches (85%+ similarity).
- Use the search box and pagination to browse results.

All Products (Batch) tab:
- Set Similarity Threshold (0.85–0.95) and Exclude Zero Price Products.
- Click Find All Similar Products (Batch) to compute and save results.
- Download the full results as JSON with the Download button.

---

### Chatbot
Purpose: Ask questions about the data in plain English.

What to do:
- Type a question and click Send (e.g., “show me 10oz beer bottles”).
- The answer appears below. Recent answers are cached so repeats are faster.

---

### Downloads & Exports
- Charts: Use the chart toolbar to download PNG images.
- Product Matching (Batch): Click “Download All Similar Products JSON”.

### If You See No Results
- Make sure at least two companies are selected for Product Matching.
- Try widening global filters (Companies / Market Segment) or clearing local filters (search, bins, thresholds).
